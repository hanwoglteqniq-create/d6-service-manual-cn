6.2.7.2 装订器 D6 Machine Dimension 和 Basic Weight 

[装订器 D6/装订器 D6 (Base 装订器)]

  • 设备 尺寸: 855 x 725 x 1204 (W x D x H mm)

  • Machine Weight: 85.0kg or lighter (当...时 the 打孔单元 已安装, the weight is increased by 6 kg)

  • 设备 尺寸: 892 x 725 x 1204 (W x D x H mm)

  • Machine Weight: 105.0 kg or lighter (当...时 the 打孔单元 已安装, the weight is increased by 6 kg)
