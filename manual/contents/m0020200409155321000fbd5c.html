6.2.5.14 TCBM/TBM 缓冲 特性 

此 特性 is intended to improve 小册子 job productivity of 装订器 D6. 当...时 a 小册子 job consisting of 两个或更多 纸张 of 纸张 is 已选择, the 1st 纸张 的 subsequent 设置 waits in the 缓冲 of 本设备 和 is transported together 使用 2nd 纸张 to 装订器 D6.

  • Productivity of 缓冲

    Productivity refers 到 productivity 当...时 IOT 送纸 纸张 at the pitch notified by the TCBM, 和 当...时 the 相同 设置 号码 is continuously used.
