6.2.3.6 SEM static elimination 特性 

Removes residual 纸张 charge 在...期间 IOT 输出, 哪个 causes 老化 of 纸张 存储 和 electric shock.

  • Static elimination 方法: 用于 高-电阻 纸张 这样的 as film, 哪个 tends to retain electric charge, a contact-类型 conductive 辊 (DC 偏压) 用于 涂抹/施加 a charge of opposite polarity 到 转印 to 拆卸 平均值 charge to 附近 zero, 和 然后 non-contact-corotron (AC 偏压) removes fine bipolar charge unevenness. As the contact 类型 conductive 辊 applies a 转印 reverse electric field, it 可以 used 用于 表面 discharge jobs.

    It 不是 compatible 和/与 侧面 2 输出 jobs.

  • Static elimination 控制: The multi-digital 开关 located 在...上 上方 前面 manually 调整 static elimination 电压 in 100 steps 从 00 to 99. As the static elimination 电压 differs 取决于 the running 纸张 电阻, 设置 an optimum 值.

    当...时 using a material 该 不 require static elimination, 设置 设置 计数 to 00 和 关闭 the static elimination 电压. Leaving the applied 电压 设置 as it is 可能 原因 a malfunction.
